NEGATIVE AND POSITIVE FINDINGS
Matched3-epoch C patch-only underperformed B on historical test:0.091816 versus0.123897. Native patches alone did not improve aggregate quality under this budget. Random crops can omit foreground in positive source images and lose whole-image context; these are plausible mechanisms from the code, not a separately proven explanation.
Phase II evolved through weighted BCE(E), present-target Dice(F/G), foreground-aware crops(H), training-frequency-weighted Dice(J), and extended training(K). Eight historical run directories,7 fresh starts plus K continuation. K shares J's first3 epochs and reaches9 total; no patching-only causal claim. Full historical table, ablations and configs remain under phase2/artifacts/.
L improved development Dice but failed K reliability safeguards. N increased BCE proportion and reduced some false positives while losing genuine recall, especially C4. O brightness/contrast jitter did not beat matched L and failed safeguards.
P added0.10 mean pairwise sigmoid overlap regularization. Its matched-L Dice gain is only0.000442; simultaneous active pixels dropped3.52%, but normal alarms rose1141->1143/1180 (K1130). C4 small-object detection remained16/36 versus K17/36. P failed the original K safeguards; it is not approved for the GUI.
All9048 native training annotation masks were verified pixel-exclusive in the P study, although307 images contain distinct classes at different pixels. Independent sigmoid inference remains; no argmax enforcement.
K Class2: development precision0.042245, recall0.118542, Dice0.062291; historical test Dice0.082752, precision0.055592, recall0.161799. These are different splits. Normal-image any-pixel alarms: validation1130/1180; historical test452/472. A few predicted pixels are not a reliable whole-product verdict.
Small-object detection means >=10% correct-class pixels recovered in an8-connected GT component<=1024 pixels; it is not instance-IoU/AP. No calibrated confidence, external-domain accuracy, coil/acquisition separation or industrial acceptance reliability is established. Single seed, limited budgets and reused validation remain limitations.
Contribution: implement and measure a resolution-aware native patch pipeline, positive-Hann reconstruction, training-only class exposure changes and controlled negative/positive comparisons. U-Net, patching, Hann windows and class weighting are established methods; no invented-algorithm or incompatible-paper superiority claim.
