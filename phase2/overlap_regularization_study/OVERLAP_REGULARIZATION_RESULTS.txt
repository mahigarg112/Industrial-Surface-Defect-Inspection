LIMITATIONS: One coefficient and one seed; development validation has been reused extensively. Historical test had already been opened previously but was not evaluated or used in this study. No independent generalization claim. Annotation exclusivity is dataset-specific, not proof that physical defects never overlap. A uniform overlap penalty may suppress legitimate/uncertain responses and recall; safeguards take precedence over Dice or attractive panels.
The auxiliary directly addresses simultaneous responses. A single wrong active channel can have almost no overlap penalty, whether it lies on background or on another GT class; the unchanged BCE/Dice supervision must still address those errors. Thresholded coactivation counts do not by themselves establish better segmentation or calibrated probabilities.
Codex assisted new auxiliary implementation, tests and analysis; original network, K loss, sampler, tiler, rendering and metrics are reused. No novel segmentation algorithm is claimed.
